## Citations

Pandoc-style citations resolve against `references.bib` when
`features.citations` is on. Example: the AFFiNE paper [@wang2022affine]
and a handbook entry [@doe2024handbook].

## Sidenotes

GFM footnotes become margin notes (or popovers on narrow viewports) when
`features.sidenotes` is enabled.[^demo]

Publishers can also author `{{note}}` / `{{sidenotes[label]: note}}` syntax
and run `transformSidenoteSyntax` before MDX sees the source — raw double
braces must not reach the MDX parser.

[^demo]: A sample sidenote. On wide layouts it sits in the margin; otherwise
  click the marker to open a popover.
